My Creative Statement

My name is Victoria Qu, and when I think about media and indie games nowadays, I am reminded of how representation for women is still overlooked everywhere, and how many of our true identities are washed away by observations from male developers. That is why I wish to tell a story that is unapologetically feminist on one half, while also challenging confucianist beliefs on the other through game design. Through it, I intend for my work to be more critical about the filial culture that has shaped years of Chinese history and sexism.
