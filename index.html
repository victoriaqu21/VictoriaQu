My Creative Statement

My name is Victoria Qu, and when I think about myself along with the media I wish to create, I always find myself circling back to my childhood as a daughter in a confucian Asian household. Especially with media and indie games nowadays, I feel representation for women in male dominant spaces is still overlooked everywhere, and many of our true identities are washed away by observations from male developers. That is why I wish to tell a story that is unapologetically feminist on one half, and challenging confucianist beliefs on the other hopefully through game design. Through it, I intend for my work to be unhateful, but more critical about the filial culture that has shaped years of Chinese history and sexism.
